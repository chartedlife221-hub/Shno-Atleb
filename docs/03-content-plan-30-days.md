# 30-Day Content Plan — Instagram Reels / TikTok

**Handles:** @shnoatleb / @shnoatleb.kw · **Goal:** saves, shares, waitlist signups, order submissions
**Format key:** 🎬 Reel/TikTok (15–30s) · 🖼 Carousel · 📖 Story/poll
**Rule for every post:** show the exact order name on screen, cite the source ("staff said" / "community pick"), and never say "official best seller." CTA is always one of: *Save this*, *Send it to who orders for you*, *Submit yours*, *Join the waitlist*.

## Measurement (weekly)

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Saves and shares per post | Strongest signal of usefulness |
| Profile → site clicks | Funnel entry |
| Waitlist signups per 100 visits | Conversion |
| Submissions received | Community-sourcing health |
| Which area / category posts win | Decides next week's mix and which items to verify first |

## Production notes

- Film at the café counter showing the typed order on screen; keep cuts fast, subtitles bilingual.
- Ask permission before filming staff or recording a café. Keep it factual, no brand logos used as endorsement.
- Reuse every Reel as a carousel and a Story for 3x reach.
